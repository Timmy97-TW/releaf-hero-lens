# ReLeaf first visual

Four studies for the first screen of the ReLeaf homepage (GEMS Taiwan, iGEM 2026).
The team walking Farmer Chen's field is the top layer and the bioreactor bench is
the layer underneath, after the two-layer flashlight hero of Marburg 2024.

Live: <https://timmy97-tw.github.io/releaf-hero-lens/>

| # | Variant | Idea |
|---|---------|------|
| 1 | [Green light](green-light/) | The cursor is a green lamp; the bench switches on under its light and the lit path closes behind it |
| 2 | [Along the line](along-the-line/) | A route under the queue; the window follows the cursor and docks past Farmer Chen |
| 3 | [Roots](roots/) | Lift the field; a root runs from each person's feet to the reactor underground |
| 4 | [Membrane](membrane/) | The farm/bench line is the reactor's membrane: cells stay below, protectant rises through |

Round one's Leaf window, Stoma and Under the field are in git history (`2cd2e0a`);
round two's more decorated versions of these four are at `4f3ebaf`. Round three is
the clean pass: centred type with a staged entrance, one frosted hint pill, spring
motion, 1px hairlines, black grounds, green only as the accent.

## Layout

```
index.html                 the gallery
<variant>/index.html       generated by tools/build_pages.py; do not edit by hand
assets/js/lens-core.js     shared engine: loads both photos, one canvas, pointer, idle
assets/js/v-<variant>.js   what each variant draws
assets/css/hero.css        nav stand-in, hero text, notes below the fold
assets/img/                farm-*.jpg|webp, bench-*.jpg|webp at 1400 and 2400 wide
```

Everything is static. Preview with `python3 -m http.server` from the repo root.

## Swapping in the new bench photograph

1. Export it at 1400 and 2400 wide, JPEG and WebP, over the current files:

   ```sh
   for w in 1400 2400; do
     magick NEW.jpg -auto-orient -resize ${w}x -strip -quality 82 assets/img/bench-$w.jpg
     cwebp -quiet -q 80 assets/img/bench-$w.jpg -o assets/img/bench-$w.webp
   done
   ```

2. Open `assets/js/lens-core.js` and update `BENCH`. Both values are fractions of
   the photograph's width and height:
   - `anchor`: the point that lands in the middle of every window. Now the vessel on
     its stand, a little above the table.
   - `core`: `[left, top, right, bottom]` of the part that must fit inside the
     window. Now the top of the stand to the stand's foot, and the stand to the pump.

   Every variant sizes its window content from `core`, so nothing else needs to change
   unless the new photograph is framed very differently.

3. `python3 tools/build_pages.py` is only needed if the notes or hints change.

## Checking a variant without moving the mouse

`?at=0.6,0.45` puts the cursor at that fraction of the hero; `?still=3` runs three
seconds of animation at once and freezes. The thumbnails on the index page were made
this way.
